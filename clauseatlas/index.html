THE PROBLEM

Government compliance was never designed to be read by one person.

Federal obligations are fragmented across the FAR, DFARS, NIST frameworks, OPM guidance, and executive orders — interlinked by cross-references and constantly changing. A single contract can carry dozens of clauses, each pulling in its own control framework, and generic GRC platforms built for SOC 2 checklists simply don't see them.

The traditional answer is consultants reading contracts line by line: weeks of billable review that misses obscure clauses and is stale the moment a regulation updates. That is the gap ClauseAtlas was built to close.

HOW IT WORKS

From contract to living compliance map.

01 · SCAN

Ingest your documents

Contracts, SOWs, RFPs, awards, and modifications — PDF, Word, or scanned. ClauseAtlas reads them the way a contracts attorney would, at machine speed.

02 · EXTRACT

Surface every clause

Every regulatory clause is identified and catalogued — including the flow-downs and cross-references buried in incorporated documents that manual review misses.

03 · MAP

Connect clauses to controls

Each clause maps to the specific controls it invokes — DFARS 252.204-7012 to the 110 controls of NIST 800-171, and on across 28 regulation families.

04 · TRACK

Watch posture live

Your compliance posture becomes a living dashboard — gaps prioritized, evidence tracked, and requirements updated as regulations change.

CAPABILITIES

Built for the regulations that gate the federal market.

Compliance matrix generation

Requirement matrices generated automatically — every identified clause mapped to its implementation obligations, evidence requirements, and audit verification standards.

Plain-language translation

Regulatory text converted into actionable guidance. Your engineering team gets an implementation roadmap, not legal language that needs an interpreter.

Gap identification

Contract requirements compared against your current posture, so remediation effort and budget go to the gaps that actually block award or authorization.

Cross-reference resolution

Federal regulation is a web, not a list. ClauseAtlas follows the cross-references — clause to framework to control — so nothing incorporated by reference gets missed.

Regulatory change monitoring

The regulatory base is maintained continuously — when a rule changes or a new requirement lands, affected obligations in your portfolio are flagged.

Audit-ready evidence

Posture tracking is organized around what assessors verify — so audit preparation is an export, not a fire drill.
