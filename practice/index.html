01QUALIFY

Pre-Qualification

The federal market is gated by technical qualifications that take most companies months — sometimes years — to satisfy. We prepare, implement, and capture those qualifications: FedRAMP, SOC 2, and the broader body of federal regulatory requirements that determines whether you are even allowed to compete.

Every engagement runs on ClauseAtlas, our compliance platform purpose-built for government regulation — so clause analysis that takes consultancies weeks happens in minutes, and your compliance posture is tracked in real time rather than rediscovered at audit.

FedRAMP SOC 2 Type I/II NIST 800-171/53 CMMC L2/L3 DoD ATO
WHAT WE DELIVER
  • Gap assessment & roadmap — current posture against the framework that gates your market, with a prioritized, sequenced remediation plan
  • Authorization pathway strategy — FedRAMP route selection, CMMC level determination, and timing decisions made with the endgame in view
  • Documentation & evidence architecture — SSPs, policies, and control evidence built the way assessors actually verify them
  • Assessor coordination — 3PAO and C3PAO engagement, mock assessments, and finding remediation through authorization
  • Continuous monitoring — posture tracked live on ClauseAtlas after the certificate, not rebuilt from scratch each cycle
02CAPTURE

Business Development

Federal capture is relationship-driven, calendar-driven, and unforgiving of missed windows. We run every phase of federal business development — from standing up a government business unit inside a commercial company, to qualifying the pipeline, to full proposal operations when the RFP drops.

This is not advisory-from-a-distance. We build the capture plan, work the relationships alongside your team, and manage proposals through color-team reviews and orals — the same discipline the incumbents use, brought to companies entering the market.

Federal BU build-out Capture management Proposal operations
WHAT WE DELIVER
  • Federal market strategy — where you actually fit: agencies, vehicles, set-asides, and the realistic path from first contract to franchise
  • Government business unit build-out — structure, processes, and hiring plans for a federal arm inside a commercial company
  • Capture management — opportunity qualification, teaming strategy, and customer engagement backed by industry relationships
  • Proposal management — volume writing, compliance matrices, color-team reviews, and oral presentation preparation
  • Pipeline discipline — bid/no-bid rigor so you spend your B&P dollars where you can win
03DELIVER

Post-Award Operations

Winning is the start, not the finish. Post-award compliance failures can unwind everything the first two phases built — and commercial teams routinely underestimate how different delivering for the government is from delivering for enterprise customers.

We keep contract operations compliant, hold service delivery to the standard the government is grading you on, and help your team internalize GovCon culture — deliverable cadences, contracting officer relationships, and the paperwork that protects your contract — without losing commercial momentum.

Compliance ops Service quality GovCon culture
WHAT WE DELIVER
  • Contract compliance operations — flow-down obligations, reporting requirements, and clause-level tracking run on ClauseAtlas
  • Continuous authorization upkeep — ConMon, POA&M management, and re-assessment readiness so qualifications never lapse
  • Service delivery quality — QASP-aligned performance management and the metrics your contracting officer actually reviews
  • GovCon operating rhythm — CDRLs, invoicing discipline, and government-customer communication norms embedded in your team
  • Growth on contract — option-year positioning, CPARS excellence, and past performance you can bid with
