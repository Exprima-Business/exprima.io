WHY EXPRIMA

Turning agency interest into contracted revenue.

Companies typically come to Exprima with a strong product, genuine interest from a government customer, and no award yet. Closing that gap is the firm's core work. We chart the fastest credible route to a first contract and execute it together — qualification, capture, and delivery in a single engagement — without the cost of standing up a government division before the revenue exists.

That work is grounded in 25 years of winning and delivering federal programs across defense, homeland security, federal health, and civilian agencies: more than 40 authorizations earned, $350M in programs delivered, and sustained work alongside the program executives, authorizing officials, and contracting officers who make the decisions. The same experience shapes every recommendation we make — including, when warranted, the recommendation not to pursue.
