The Differentiator

Built on Upstream Risk Translation

Exprima applies systematic frameworks created by founder Elliott Mattice over 25 years of federal program leadership. These frameworks convert early-stage signals into strategic intelligence while decision margin still exists.

Framework 01

Policy Translation Method

Read regulatory intent 6-12 months before publication

Most organizations wait for final regulations before preparing. Policy Translation Method reads upstream signals in draft guidance, comment periods, and legislative language to identify enforcement priorities early.

Applied to Client Work:

When preparing defense contractors for CMMC certification, signals from DoD guidance memos six months prior reveal priority enforcement areas. Compliance programs are configured proactively using Clause Atlas tool to scan for these anticipated requirements, creating 6-12 month preparation advantage.

Framework 02

Geopolitical Risk Posture

Assess vendor and supply chain through state actor lens

Traditional vendor risk focuses on SOC2 reports and security questionnaires. Geopolitical Risk Posture adds analysis of supply chain dependencies, export control signals, and procurement policy shifts driven by strategic competition.

Applied to Client Work:

When assessing healthcare clients' AI model providers, analysis includes data sovereignty concerns, export control implications, and alternative vendor availability. ZeroTrusted.ai platform monitors model dependencies and flags geopolitical risk exposure in real-time.

Framework 03

Incentive Analysis for Control Design

Design controls aligned with stakeholder motivations

Generic compliance templates fail audits and create operational friction. Incentive Analysis designs controls based on how auditors actually verify, how engineers actually work, and what executives actually prioritize—maximizing both audit success and operational acceptance.

Applied to Client Work:

When implementing AI governance programs, ZeroTrusted.ai platform policies are configured to align with team workflows rather than blocking them. Shadow AI is redirected through governed channels instead of prohibited outright. Result: 90%+ audit pass rates with high operational adoption.
